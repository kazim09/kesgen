How the waiting list works

Requests are handled in the order they are received.

  • The first four people will each be offered one of the available knives.
  • You cannot choose a specific knife or serial number. I will allocate one of the remaining knives.
  • One entry per person.
  • I will contact interested buyers in order by email or Instagram DM.
  • If someone passes or doesn’t reply in 24 hours, I’ll contact the next person.
